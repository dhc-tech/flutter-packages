# Code of Conduct

We want participation in this project to be welcoming, respectful and
harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience, background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Assume good intent and give people room to learn.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks or insults.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Sustained disruption of discussions or reviews.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, reject or remove comments, commits, issues and pull
requests that violate this code, and may ban contributors who do so repeatedly.
Report violations privately to the maintainers through a
[GitHub security advisory](https://github.com/dhc-tech/flutter-packages/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting [@dhc-tech](https://github.com/dhc-tech). Reports are handled
confidentially.

This code is adapted from the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
